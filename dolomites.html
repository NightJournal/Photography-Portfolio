Trip Overview

Start in the eastern Dolomites for Cadini, Tre Cime and Misurina, then transfer west for Seceda, Passo Giau and Alpe di Siusi.

Route

Start From Venice

Collect the car at Venice Marco Polo Airport and drive east first. It is closer to Misurina and Tre Cime than Ortisei.

Bases

2 Nights East + 2 Nights West

Use an east base for Cadini, Tre Cime and Misurina. Use a west base for Seceda, Giau and Siusi.

Priority

Cadini + Passo Giau

If weather or fatigue forces cuts, protect Cadini sunrise and Passo Giau night photography first.

Parking and Access

Auronzo

Rifugio Auronzo

Use for Cadini sunrise and the short Tre Cime visit. Book or verify early access before travel.

Astro

Lago di Misurina

Car-close shoreline shooting. Good for testing night settings with lower risk.

Night

Passo Giau

Arrive before dark, stay close to parking, and avoid improvising on unfamiliar terrain at midnight.

Sunset

Seceda

Requires lift access from Ortisei. Confirm prices and last lift-down time before committing to sunset.

Sunrise

Alpe di Siusi

Private car access is restricted. Check reservation and early-entry rules close to the trip.

Rule

No Midnight Guesswork

Mountain roads, gates and parking systems are not friendly to last-minute night decisions.

Photography Guide

Baseline settings and shot priorities for the most important locations.

Cadini

Sunrise Method

Arrive before light. Use layered jagged peaks as silhouettes, then expose for highlights as alpenglow appears.

Misurina

Astro Method

Use shoreline reflections, face south or southwest, and keep the car close for a low-risk night shoot.

Giau

Milky Way Method

Use a wide-angle lens. Start around 16mm, f/2.8, 13 seconds and ISO 4000, then adjust from test frames.

Safety and Cut List

If Tired

Drop In This Order

  1. Alpe di Siusi sunrise after Passo Giau astro.
  2. Extra Tre Cime walking beyond the short Lavaredo route.
  3. Misurina astro if weather is poor.

Keep Cadini sunrise and Passo Giau astro if conditions allow.

Packing

Do Not Forget

  • Headlamp and spare battery
  • Warm layer, gloves and hat
  • Tripod and remote timer
  • Offline maps and trail routes
  • Power bank and spare camera batteries
  • Water and snacks before sunrise or astro
  • Lens cloth for dew
  • Daily weather, lift and road checks
