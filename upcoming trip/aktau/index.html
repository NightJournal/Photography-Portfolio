Photography Notes

Camera Settings

Lens: 16-35mm; 100-400mm

For desert scenes, keep shutter speed high enough to avoid vibration from wind and hand movement.

White balance can stay warm for sunrise and a little cooler for the coast and blue hour.


Airakty–Shomanai · N44°13′23″, E52°13′28″

Milky Way

Direction: S–SW · 180°–205°

Composition: fixed position; castle-like rocks as foreground; keep the southern low horizon open.

For panoramas: overlap by 30–50%.

For star trails (30 mins): M mode · f/2.8 · Shutter 8 sec · ISO 6400–8000 · Interval 10 sec · WB 4000K
