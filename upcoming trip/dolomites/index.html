Trip Overview

Route

Start From East

Collect car at Venice Marco Polo. Airport is closer to Misurina / Tre Cime than to Ortisei. Begin east, then transfer west.

Bases

East: 2 nights + West: 2 nights

East base for Cadini, Tre Cime, Misurina. West base for Seceda, Giau and Siusi.

Priority

Cadini + Giau

Prioritize Cadini sunrise and Passo Giau milky way first.

Parking and access

Auronzo

Rifugio Auronzo

Book earliest morning entry around 05:00. Budget €40. Use it for Cadini sunrise and short Tre Cime visit in one window.

Safe astro

Lago di Misurina

Car-close shooting. Budget €0–10 depending on exact parking spot. Best for testing astro settings.

Main astro

Passo Giau

Roadside parking is generally free. Drive before full darkness, then wait for the Milky Way window.

Sunset

Seceda

Requires Ortisei lift + parking. Confirm last lift-down time before relying on sunset.

Sunrise

Alpe di Siusi

Private car access is restricted; early entry is the key. Check 2026 P1/P2 reservation requirements.

Rule

Do not improvise at midnight

Mountain access, gates and parking systems are not designed for random midnight entry.

Accommodation

East · 2 nights

Misurina / Dobbiaco / Cortina

Best for plan: Misurina if not overpriced.

Best value: Dobbiaco/Toblach guesthouses.

Convenience: Cortina, but usually more expensive in July.

Filters: free parking, late check-in, flexible cancellation, breakfast or kitchen access.

West · 2 nights

Siusi / Castelrotto / Ortisei

Best for Seceda: Ortisei.

Best budget/value: Siusi or Castelrotto.

Avoid: staying too far in Bolzano/Bressanone unless price difference is large.

Filters: parking, short drive to Alpe di Siusi access, easy evening food.

Photography Notes

Camera settings

16mm f/2.8

Start with: Exposure 8s · ISO 1600.

Shoot 10-20 identical foreground frames for stacking. Manual focus on a bright star.

White balance: 3500K–4000K.


Cadini

Sunrise

Arrive 1 hour before sunrise.

AV mode · f/8-11 (16 for sunstar) · ISO Auto: 100–400.

Time Interval: 3-5 sec · Manual focus

Misurina, Giau

Astro

Face south/southwest.

M mode · Time Interval: 10 secs · Manual focus

For panoramas, overlap by 30–50%.

Reminders

If tired or cloudy

Drop in this order

  1. Siusi sunrise if you are too tired after Giau astro.
  2. Extra Tre Cime walking beyond the short Lavaredo route.
  3. Misurina astro if weather is poor.

Keep if possible: Cadini sunrise and Passo Giau astro.

Packing

Do not forget

  • Headlamp + portable charger
  • Warm layer, gloves, hat
  • Tripod
  • Offline maps and trail routes
  • spare camera batteries
  • Water + snack before sunrise/astro
  • Lens cloth
  • Daily weather/lift/road checks
